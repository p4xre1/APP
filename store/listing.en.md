## Full description

```
Fatorati is a small-business manager that works entirely on your phone: invoices, estimates, expenses, customers and a subscriptions tracker — with no account and no internet connection.

EVERYTHING STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE
Fatorati is built without the internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no sign-up, no cloud, no analytics, no advertising. You create a 6-digit PIN on first launch and your records are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Screenshots and recent-app previews are blocked by default.

INVOICES AND ESTIMATES
Create professional documents with your own logo, business details, tax number, items, discounts, notes and payment terms. Every document keeps its own tax rate and currency, numbers are sequential (PREFIX-YEAR-0001), and you can export a PDF and share it with any app on your phone.

CUSTOMERS, PRODUCTS, PROJECTS AND EXPENSES
Keep your client list, reuse products and services, track what each project earns, and record expenses against a category. The dashboard shows revenue, expenses and outstanding amounts per currency, with charts.

SUBSCRIPTIONS AND REMINDERS
Track recurring services with their billing cycle, payment method and renewal date. Fatorati shows what is due soon, totals each currency separately, exports the list to Excel or PDF, and can remind you with a local notification — reminders are scheduled on your device and you can turn them off at any time.

YOUR MONEY, YOUR CURRENCIES
Totals are never mixed: each currency is summed on its own, both monthly and yearly. Amounts are stored exactly, so there is no rounding drift in your books.

BACKUP AND EXPORT
Export an encrypted .fatorati backup (AES-256-GCM with a password you choose) and import it on another phone. Export customers, invoices, expenses and subscriptions to CSV, Excel or PDF. Note that those CSV/PDF/Excel files are not encrypted, so delete them when you are done with them.

TAX ASSISTANT AND TAX GUIDE
Fatorati includes general tax information for Morocco, France, Spain, Portugal and the United States, with field hints where you type invoice details and a guide with a "last reviewed" date. This is general information, not tax advice: check the current rules with your accountant or the tax authority before relying on them.

SECURITY
- 6-digit PIN, verifier only stored — the PIN itself is never kept
- AES-256-GCM encryption with PBKDF2-SHA256 (600,000 iterations)
- Optional biometric unlock
- Screenshot and recent-apps protection on by default
- Two-step confirmation before anything is deleted
- No PIN recovery: keep an exported backup, because a forgotten PIN cannot be recovered by anyone

FIVE LANGUAGES
Arabic (full right-to-left layout), English, French, Spanish and Portuguese, with your choice of currency, date and number format.

FREE
No subscription, no ads, no account, no in-app purchases, no tracking.

Fatorati is an offline business tool. It is not affiliated with, certified by or approved by any tax authority, and it does not file anything for you.
```
